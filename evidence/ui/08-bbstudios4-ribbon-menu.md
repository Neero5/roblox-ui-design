# 08 bbstudios4 ribbon menu

**Owner, 2026-11-14:** 4: "It's great but probably wouldn't fit aesthetically and mechanically for most of my game ideas."

![[ui08-ribbon-menu.jpg]]

- **What:** a full-screen menu in one blue family. A dark silhouette of the character on the left, stacked menu words on the right in tilted, uneven cut-out letters (each word at its own angle and size), and wide light-blue ribbons that sweep across the screen continuously. The hovered word gets its own ribbon behind it, which slides to the new word as the selection moves (a lavender ribbon at 14.9 to 16.6 s).
- **Motion:** ribbons flow slowly and endlessly (idle life is the backdrop, not the buttons); the selection ribbon glides from word to word.
- **Identity:** the strongest type-as-identity piece in the set: the letters themselves carry the style (a stylised, persona-like look).
- **Works:** one hue family; the selection is unmistakable without a box (L10); the character as the still anchor (R6).
- **Risk:** constant full-screen ribbon motion breaks R7 (idle motion only in the hero) unless the genre allows it; the tilted letters cost legibility at small sizes (R13).
