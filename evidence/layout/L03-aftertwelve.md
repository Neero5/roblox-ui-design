# L03 Aftertwelve (from clip 04)

**Owner, 2026-11-10:** clip 04 rated 4.5 ("its RPG identity pops up immedately and catches the eye"); its layouts named as a reference.

![[L03-aftertwelve.jpg]]

## Layout
- **Start screen:** a huge, widely tracked serif title over a clock dial ornament, a tagline ("THE HOUR AWAITS"), a menu of three items numbered I, II, III, a thin tick-mark ruler at the bottom-left; the character fills the right half.
- **Every step screen shares one skeleton:** a tracked caps title top-left with a short subtitle and a tiny diamond on a hairline; content in the left half; "◆ BACK" bottom-left; the character on the right, never moving (L4, R6).
- **Slot select = list + detail:** three slot cards numbered I, II, III on the left, a detail card on the right ("EMPTY HOUR" with labelled rows) and its button.
- **Origin = stacked choice cards:** title, a one-line flavour line in italics, two bullet perks; the chosen card has a lit edge.
- **Satchel:** a pocket-watch dial holds the equipment slots around the avatar (head, torso, weapon, trousers on the hour marks); a search field, underlined category tabs and a dark grid on the right; the selected item's name and stats under the grid with angled EQUIP and DROP buttons.

## Principles beyond L1 to L7 (adopted 2026-11-10 as L8 to L10 and the L4 extension: "Adopt them all")
- **The signature motif comes from the game's NAME:** "Aftertwelve" -> clocks, hours, roman numerals. The dial is the start screen ornament, the slot numbering and the inventory itself. This is the strongest identity move in the whole set (I1).
- **One skeleton for a flow:** every step of a flow uses the same title spot, content side, back spot and anchor.
- **Browse = selection beside a big detail** (slot list + detail card; grid + item block).
- **Quiet selected state:** a lit edge or an underline, not a fill.

## Weak
Thin pale text on bright snow (R13); hairline buttons nearly vanish at small sizes; character creation is denser and plainer than the rest.
