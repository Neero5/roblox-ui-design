# L02 Bladebound (from clip 03)

**Owner, 2026-11-10:** clip 03 rated 4 ("Elden Ring vibes ... somewhat of a AAA feel with its looks and matching identities"); "clip 03, 04 and 07's UI layouts and design could also serve as reference, I take a liking to them".

![[L02-bladebound.jpg]]

## Layout
- **Title menu:** wordmark top-left with a tiny tagline above it ("THE REALM AWAITS") and a short rule under it; a left column of five menu items, each with a diamond bullet (filled when chosen) and a faint index on the right; the world (a sword in tall grass) fills the right two thirds. Nothing else on screen.
- **Panels open OVER the menu,** which stays visible and dimmed on the left: the player never loses their place (L4, R6).
- **Store = preview + list:** a tall preview card on the left (the selected item's art, a "PREVIEW" tag, name, type, one line, a "COMING SOON" button) and a list on the right (icon, name, a small gold type tag, one line of description, a framed price). Selecting a row changes the preview; nothing pops up.
- **Choose Your Hero:** cards fanned in a row, the chosen one framed and in front, the others stepping back and darker; one wide button under them.
- **Frame language:** dark brown glass, a 1 px light border, a diamond medallion with a letter at the top centre, small diamond studs at the corners. The same frame on every panel (L1, L6).

## Principles beyond L1 to L7
- A **signature motif from the game's premise** (blade-shaped diamonds and studs for a sword game), repeated on every panel.
- **Browse = selection beside a big detail** (preview card + list), never a popup per item.
- **The selected state is quiet but unmistakable:** a filled diamond, a lighter frame, the chosen card in front; no big colour fill.

## Weak
Small text and the price boxes are hard to read; the fanned hero cards crowd the right edge.
