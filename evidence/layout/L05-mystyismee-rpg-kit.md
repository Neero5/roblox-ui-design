# L05 @mystyismee RPG UI kit

**Owner, 2026-11-14:** "Here are more UI references I'd rate around 4.5, they're great for my upcoming rpg game i wanna make".
**Scope:** an RPG-genre reference, not a universal default (the owner's own note on clip 08 and L01: a style must fit the game).

Contact sheet: ![[00-contact-sheet.jpg]] (all 19 frames in `media/L05-mystyismee/`)

## What is in it
- **Bar sets (health, mana, stamina, XP)**, each a full set in its own theme: skulls and chains with barbed wire (02-03), crimson with ornate corners (04, 19), vines dripping over a red bar (06), kitsune masks on the bar ends with an icy filigree (09, 14), a bronze sun-disc frame with crossed swords (15), pastel bars in silver filigree (12), slanted bars with colour swatches (07), pixel filigree (16).
- **Layered bars:** one wide main bar (health) with thinner bars stacked above and below it (stamina, mana, XP), all sharing one frame.
- **Hotbar slots** matching each bar set: same frame, same corner ornaments, a small keycap tab on top.
- **Title logos** (Kakugan, Heroes of Dawn, Re:Legacy): one strong treatment each (blood splatter, frosted silver, a purple void with reaching hands).
- **Danger banners** ("DANGERED / Hold your ground and don't die", "YOU ARE IN DANGER! FIGHT TO SURVIVE"): an emblem plus two lines of type at the top centre.
- **Icon set** (wings, mushroom, fangs, paw, feathers, a default portrait) in silver line art on one tile frame.
- **In game:** Ashen Overture's inventory (a silver filigree frame round a grid, an italic serif menu "-Profile- -Inventory- -Stats- -Party- -Codes-" fading by distance from the chosen one, the blurred world behind); a character creation screen (two silver-cornered pale panels, the character in the centre, one rose Finalize button bottom right).

## Principles (proposed for the owner's ruling)
1. **A theme is a full set.** Bars, hotbar slots, frames and the danger banner share one ornament family and palette; a new theme redraws all of them together (L1, L6, L8).
2. **Ornament lives on the ends and the corners**, never across the fill: the value stays readable (skull caps, masks, sun discs, corner studs).
3. **Hierarchy by bar size:** the main bar widest and centred, secondary bars thinner and shorter, stacked against it.
4. **The selected menu item is full strength, the rest fade by distance** (Ashen Overture's menu; L10's quiet selected state).
5. **The HUD hugs the bottom centre and the edges; the centre of the screen stays clear for play** (L3, L7).

## Weak or sloppy
- Typos shipped in the art: "YOU LIFE IS AT RISK", "Dispand", "DANGERED" (R12).
- Grey-on-grey type and thin italic serif text are hard to read at Roblox sizes (R13).
- Some sets carry heavy decoration (skulls plus chains plus stars); fine for a title, too much for a bar seen all match long (I3).
