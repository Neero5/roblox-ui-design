## Reading (Claude)
- The skill changed behaviour (scope, timing, cleanup, tiers, speed: 10 vs 25 min) but not the look: both sessions had the same profile vocabulary (snowflake, hexagon, ice) and only built-in assets, so they reached for the same shapes.
- A's slam wins on one thing the rules only imply: a single bright, crisp hero shape (the glowing hexagon). B's slam has many mid-value translucent crystals and no bright core, and a dark glyph the X5 check should have caught.
- Missing in the skill: (1) a reference step (pick 1 to 3 rated frames that match the effect, and compare the peak frame against them); (2) a hero-shape requirement for the peak (one bright, high-contrast shape); (3) an asset step (looks are capped by built-in textures and meshes).
